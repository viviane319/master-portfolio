# Microsite

[`index.html`](index.html): a simple single-page personal site. It is black and white with one blue accent (`#2F4BFF`), set in Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono.

- **Hero:** "I help good orgs talk to their communities." The headline sits next to a live three.js network of an org and its six communities, with messages flowing both ways. You can drag it to rotate it.
- **01 At a glance:** four headline numbers.
- **02 Selected work:** four case studies, each described as situation, what I did, and result.
- **03 How I work:** listen first, say it plainly, move together.
- **04 Jargon decompiler:** paste a mission statement to get its Flesch reading ease score, highlighted jargon and suggested swaps. It runs entirely in the browser, with no AI.
- **05 Experience:** every role, newest first.
- **06 Toolkit:** skills and tools.
- **07 More:** links to the rest of the portfolio.

three.js loads from cdnjs. If WebGL isn't available, the figure is hidden.
