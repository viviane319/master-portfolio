Approach

Community sets the story. Plain language carries it.

Missions are getting more complex and priorities keep snowballing, so the way we talk about the work has to get simpler. Every project runs through the same three moves, whether it's a single campaign or a whole brand.

01

Listen first

Start with the people the work is for: members, donors, customers. Surveys, advisory boards, and listening sessions tell you what actually lands.

At eimmigration: a customer advisory board, referral program, and listening initiatives that turn lawyer insight into messaging.

02

Say it plainly

Cut the jargon until the mission makes sense on the first read. One clear story, told consistently across every channel and brand.

At Donors of Color Network: brand strategy, visual identity, and key messaging for four separate brands.

03

Move people together

Stories only matter if they lead to action. Build the campaign, the coalition, and the press moment that get people to move.

At DOCN: the Climate Funders Justice Pledge campaign, which helped move $120M to BIPOC-led climate groups.

Values

The way I work, written down.

PEOPLE FIRST

Put people at the center

Every strategy starts with the people the organization exists for. Storytelling and community building make industry more human, accessible, and connected.

CLARITY

No jargon, no TL;DR

If a mission statement has to be read twice, it isn't done yet. Good missions deserve comms that are memorable and undeniable.

COMMUNITY

Do the hard things together

The most ambitious work gets imagined and achieved in community, with coalitions, cohorts, and partners leaning on one another.

EVIDENCE

Let data serve the story

Surveys, donor data, and media measurement tell you what's landing. The numbers point the way; the story does the moving.

RESPONSIBILITY

Opportunity comes with responsibility

What I'm able to do comes with a responsibility to our communities, and I want to work with people who feel that too.

GROUNDING

Stay rooted

Training in meditation, Eastern massage, and altar building shapes a holistic, global approach, including spaces like climate-grief gatherings.
