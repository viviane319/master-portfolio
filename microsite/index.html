Selected work.

Donors of Color Network · climate philanthropy

Pushing climate funders to resource BIPOC-led groups

What I did. Campaign content, funder-facing storytelling, and data-driven outreach with TargetSmart and Blackbaud.

$120M moved in two years, and a shift in how top climate funders give.

Donors of Color Network · brand

GG+A · nonprofit consulting

Data-driven advice for 25+ nonprofits

Situation. Nonprofits needed to know what was landing with donors, and COVID-19 had thrown fundraising off.

What I did. Surveys and research on campaign performance and messaging, plus an industry-wide report on COVID-19's impact on fundraising.

25+ clients got recommendations grounded in their own donor data.

eimmigration · legal tech

Turning immigration lawyers into advocates

Situation. A legal-tech brand that needs to be trusted inside the immigration law community.

What I did. A referral program, a customer advisory board, events, listening sessions, a review program, and field marketing.

Customers vouch for the brand, and what they say feeds back into the messaging.

03 · how I work

Listen first

Start with the people the work is for. Surveys, advisory boards, and listening sessions show what lands.

Say it plainly

Cut the jargon until the mission makes sense on the first read, then tell it the same way everywhere.

Move together

Stories matter when they lead to action: the campaign, the coalition, the press moment that gets people to move.

04 · try it

The jargon decompiler.

Paste a mission statement and run it. It scores how easy it is to read (Flesch reading ease, 0–100, higher is easier) and flags the words getting in the way. It runs in your browser; no AI.
