VIV(1)Community Signal Lab ManualVIV(1)
NAME
viv — narrative strategist, community mobilizer
SYNOPSIS
viv [--listen-first] [--say-it-plainly] [--move-together] mission
DESCRIPTION
Takes a complex mission and returns a story the people it serves can repeat after one read. Works at the intersection of philanthropy, policy, and tech. Runs the same three passes on a single campaign or a whole brand.
OPTIONS
--listen-first Start with the people the work is for: members, donors, customers. Surveys, advisory boards, and listening sessions show what lands. e.g. the eimmigration customer advisory board and referral program. --say-it-plainly Cut the jargon until the mission makes sense on the first read. One clear story, told the same way across every channel and brand. e.g. key messaging for four Donors of Color Network brands. --move-together Stories only matter if they lead to action: the campaign, the coalition, the press moment that gets people to move. e.g. the Climate Funders Justice Pledge, $120M in two years.
VALUES
people-first put the people the org exists for at the center clarity no jargon, no TL;DR community do the hard things together evidence let data serve the story responsibility opportunity comes with responsibility grounding stay rooted (meditation, Eastern massage, altar building)
