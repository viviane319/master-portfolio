Plate II

Three laws of how I work.

First law

Listen first.

Start with the people the work is for. Surveys, advisory boards, and listening sessions show what lands.

Second law

Say it plainly.

Cut the jargon until the mission makes sense on the first read, then tell it the same way everywhere.

Third law

Move together.

Stories matter when they lead to action: the campaign, the coalition, the press moment that gets people to move.
